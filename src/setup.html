🎁 Universal Claimer — Set up

Two permissions, asked for here rather than from the toolbar popup: Firefox can open a popup's permission prompt behind the popup itself, where it cannot be clicked.

1. Read your service sessions

Cookies for Twitch, Epic Games, Microsoft and Amazon's storefronts only — the browser enforces that list. They are only ever read when you ask for them.

2. Your Universal Claimer instance

Lets the connect button on your instance's own page do everything, with no trip to this extension. Sessions are only ever sent to an address you allow here.
